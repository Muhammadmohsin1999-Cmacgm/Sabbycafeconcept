The SABBY’S Experience

A Little Moment Worth Staying For.

Located on 22nd Street in Al Khobar Al Shamalia, SABBY’S brings together specialty coffee culture, authentic regional tea traditions, and a daily artisanal bakery under one calm, meticulously designed roof.

From woven cane armchairs, Sadu-inspired geometric wall art, and warm pendant lighting to our outdoor garden patio and the aroma of cinnamon dough fresh from the oven, every corner invites you to slow down.

Every Detail. Every Moment.
